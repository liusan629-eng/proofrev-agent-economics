# OSS Dependency Dividend Strategy

This READY package is designed as a reusable open-source dependency.

Possible future monetization channels after genuine independent adoption:
1. Dependency sponsorship.
2. Enterprise maintainer programs.
3. Paid hosted API/support around the MIT core.

Do not count downloads, stars, self-created dependencies, test traffic, or self-pay as revenue.
Publishing, account terms, 2FA, identity/tax onboarding, and payout setup remain human-controlled.
